Interview Preparation Tips

Your guide to mastering both technical and behavioral rounds to secure your placement.

Technical Round Focus

  • Practice Data Structures & Algorithms: Be ready to code on a whiteboard or a shared screen.
  • Review Core Subjects: Brush up on OS, DBMS, Networking, and OOP concepts thoroughly.
  • Explain Your Projects: Know the architecture, challenges, and design decisions of every project on your resume.
  • Think Aloud: Always verbalize your thought process when solving a problem—interviewers value your approach as much as the solution.

HR & Behavioral Round Focus

  • Prepare STAR Method Stories: Use the Situation, Task, Action, Result (STAR) technique for answering behavioral questions like "Tell me about a time you failed."
  • Research the Company: Know their recent projects, values, and the role's responsibilities to show genuine interest.
  • Formulate Your Strengths & Weaknesses: Frame your weakness as a challenge you are actively overcoming.
  • Prepare Questions to Ask: Always have 2-3 thoughtful questions ready for the interviewer about the team, culture, or future projects.
